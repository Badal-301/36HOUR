Act as a senior UI/UX designer, full-stack developer, frontend architect, and product designer. Design and develop a complete, polished, production-quality hackathon website for a college technology club associated with Google Developer communities.

Do not just create a visual mockup or a simple static landing page. Build a functional, responsive website with realistic content, clear navigation, working interactions, a registration flow, and clean, organized code.

The project should look impressive during a technical club evaluation and demonstrate creativity, problem-solving, usability, functionality, responsiveness, and thoughtful technical decisions.

7:00 PM–12:00 AM: Night Sprint — implementation and experimentation. Hour 10–15.

7:00 AM–8:00 AM: Breakfast and refresh break. Hour 22–23.

8:00 AM–12:00 PM: Build Sprint 2 — core features and integrations. Hour 23–27.

12:00 PM–1:00 PM: Lunch. Hour 27–28.
